# Subcontracting Status

Foreman's post-hackathon expansion story is to hire specialist ASPs when a launch component needs one.

For the hackathon MVP, subcontracting is a single optional proof point, not a dependency.

## Current Status

Unknown. The OKX.AI A2A flow must be checked after Agentic Wallet login and ASP onboarding.

## Safe Claim

Foreman can claim subcontracting only after a real listed ASP is hired, paid through OKX.AI escrow or the supported OKX.AI payment flow, and the receipt is attached to a real customer order.

## Unsafe Claim

Foreman must not claim automated subcontracting, paid sub-agent work, or agent-to-agent procurement before that receipt exists.
