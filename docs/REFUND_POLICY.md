Locked warranty sentence:

> If the delivered asset misses the written scope and we cannot fix it within the revision window, we refund the service fee.

## Policy

- Every order starts with a written scope.
- Every order includes one revision if the delivered asset misses the written scope.
- A refund applies only when the written scope is missed and Foreman cannot fix it within the revision window.
- A refund does not apply to requests outside the accepted scope.
- Foreman does not promise marketplace approval, sales, social traction, or hackathon results.
